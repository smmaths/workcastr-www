Your work in three tabs: Timeline, Reports, and Goals

See upcoming plans and recent logs together on the Timeline, build and share Reports by period, and set time-bound Goals tied to projects—with dashboards, progress gauges, and optional share links. Rich descriptions, calendar imports, and @ # ~ mentions stay at the center of how you capture work.

Built around how you actually work

A clear three-tab layout on desktop, with the same three destinations in the mobile bottom bar—plus the power features you expect underneath.

📋
Timeline

One unified view for upcoming plans, today, and recent activity. Log work, convert plans to entries, and keep context without jumping between screens.

📊
Reports

Create reports, move through weeks or months, and refresh previews on demand. Share a read-only link so clients or stakeholders can view results without an account.

🎯
Goals

Define time-bound targets by #project—total hours, percent complete, or a numeric track (e.g. weight, savings). Open a dashboard with gauges and drill into history; open a chart on any target to see reported values vs. the ideal line over time. Share the dashboard with a link on Pro and Business.

@
Mentions & quick signals

Tag @people, #projects, and ~tags in descriptions. Add +hours, +N% on a project for progress, or =N with a project for numeric milestones—Workcastr parses them and ties them to goals and reporting.

📅
Integrations

Connect Google Calendar, Outlook, iCal, Todoist and more. Import information from external sources as log entries automatically.

🔁
Share reports & goal dashboards

Share reports and (on Pro and Business) read-only goal dashboards with a simple link—viewers do not need a Workcastr account.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Workcastr supports Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook. Beginner includes one connected calendar; Pro and Business include unlimited calendar connections. Import events as planned items and time entries automatically.

Workcastr uses per-user data isolation. Your time entries, plans, projects, people, tags, and goals are private to your account unless you explicitly create a share link.

Timeline combines upcoming work, today, and recent history so you can plan and log in one place. Reports let you define views over your data by week or month and share them by link. Goals are time-bound targets tied to projects (hours, percent complete, or a numeric value); you get a dashboard with gauges, per-target history charts, and optional read-only sharing on Pro and Business. On smaller screens, the same three areas appear in the bottom navigation.

Tag people (@name), projects (#project), and tags (~tag) in descriptions—Workcastr creates and organizes them. Use +1.5 (or similar) for hours, +25% with a project for percent progress toward a goal, and =220 with a project for numeric milestones (e.g. a scale reading). These tie naturally into the Goals tab when your targets match those project mentions.
